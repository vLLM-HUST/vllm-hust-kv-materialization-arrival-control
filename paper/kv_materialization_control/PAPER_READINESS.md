# Paper Readiness

## Decision

**GO for paper convergence as a short, evidence-bounded negative result.**

**NO-GO for a positive acceleration claim or a broad full systems paper.** The
controller loses to `always_full_reuse` in the preregistered main matrix, and no
promoted catalog workload confirms the preregistered 5% benefit threshold.

## Contribution That Survives Review

1. Requested, effective, and engine-realized KV actions are distinct measurable
   quantities; reporting only the policy output can overstate partial reuse.
2. A block-aligned segmented-tail carrier exposes the lookup, alignment, commit,
   isolation, and recomputed-tail costs needed for a physical break-even test.
3. When local full reuse is already cheap, the tested action selector has no
   measured economic headroom: full reuse wins 6/6 main rounds and 20/20 added
   confirmation rounds.
4. The paper states its reproducibility boundary explicitly: 39 lifecycles have
   retained raw bundles and 102 are aggregate-only supporting evidence.
